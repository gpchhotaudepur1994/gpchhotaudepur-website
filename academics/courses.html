Courses

Government Polytechnic, Chhotaudepur offers diploma programmes designed to develop technical knowledge, practical skills, and industry readiness. Each programme combines classroom learning with laboratory work, practical training, and project-based learning.

Students can pursue employment in relevant industries after completing their diploma or continue their education through applicable higher-study pathways.

Automobile Engineering

What You Will Study

Students learn about automobile engines, vehicle systems, transmission and mechanisms, automobile electrical systems, manufacturing processes, materials, thermodynamics, and related engineering fundamentals. The programme develops practical skills relevant to vehicle maintenance, servicing, manufacturing, and automotive systems.

Career Opportunities

Automobile manufacturing, vehicle servicing and maintenance, workshops, dealerships, automotive component industries, and related technical roles.

Higher Studies

After completing the diploma, students may pursue applicable higher-study pathways in Automobile Engineering or related engineering disciplines, subject to eligibility and admission rules.

Civil Engineering

What You Will Study

Students learn about construction, surveying, structural mechanics, hydraulics, construction technology, engineering materials, and other fundamentals of civil engineering. The programme develops practical skills related to construction activities, surveying, site work, and civil engineering applications.

Construction companies, infrastructure projects, surveying, site supervision, consulting organisations, and government or public-sector projects.

Mechanical Engineering

What You Will Study

The programme develops practical skills in manufacturing, production, maintenance, design, and mechanical engineering applications.
