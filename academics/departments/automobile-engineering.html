Department Vision

To be the source of creation of the best technocrat who are meant for automobile industries, society & government

Department Mission

  • M1: Effective implementation of outcome based curriculum for automobile engineering.
  • M2: Arrangements of training, placement, Co-curricular & extra-curricular activities for the best technocrat in automobile engineering.
  • M3: Continuous assessment, review & updating of curriculum for automobile engineering
  • M4: Generation of well-trained staffs & faculties in automobile engineering.
  • M5: Interaction with automobile industries, Society & Government to identify their needs and Implementation of their policies.
