Mechanical Engineering

About the Department

The Mechanical Engineering Department is a vibrant academic unit committed to delivering strong fundamentals, practical skills, and industry-relevant exposure in core and emerging areas of mechanical engineering. With a focus on outcome-based education, the department nurtures competent diploma engineers who are ready for higher studies, entrepreneurship, and professional practice. The department is supported by a dedicated team of 12 GES Class-II Lecturers, 3 Instructors (Grade B), and 1 Class-IV staff member, ensuring effective teaching–learning, laboratory supervision, and smooth departmental operations. Our faculty members actively engage in academic development and continuous upskilling; several are currently pursuing their Ph.D. from reputed institutes such as IITs and NITs, which strengthens our teaching quality and research culture. To promote hands-on learning and innovation, the department houses a 3D Printer Laboratory, enabling students to experience rapid prototyping and product development practices. Through laboratory work, mini-projects, industry visits, and technical activities, students gain exposure to design, manufacturing, thermal sciences, and modern tools. The Mechanical Engineering Department continuously strives to create an environment that encourages technical excellence, creativity, and professional ethics, supporting students in becoming skilled technocrats who can contribute effectively to industry and society.

Department Vision

To deliver competent technocrats and entrepreneur in Mechanical Engineering field to cater ever changing demand of industry and society

Department Mission

  • M-1 To prepare a student with integrative educational experiences that blends fundamentals and problem solving skills.
  • M-2 To Provides opportunities to promote organizational and leadership skill in student through various extra-curricular and co-curricular activity.
  • M-3 To develop alliances with industry and alumni for teaching, learning and placement practices.
  • M-4 Imbibe ethical principles, understanding of human nature, social awareness in students to serve the society and Sustainable development.
  • M-5 Get with program facilities and effective delivery of high quality of education by qualified teachers that ensures lifelong learning.

Program Outcomes

  1. Basic and Discipline specic knowledge: Apply knowledge of basic mathematics, science and engineering fundamentals and engineering specialization to solve the engineering problems.
  2. Problem analysis: Identify and analyze well-dened engineering problems using codified standard methods.
  3. Design/ development of solutions: Design solutions for well-dened technical problems: for and assist with the design of systems components or processes to meet specied needs.
  4. Engineering Tools, Experimentation and Testing: Apply tools appropriate technique to conduct standard tests and measurements.
  5. Engineering practices for society, sustainability and environment: Apply appropriate technology in context of society, sustainability, environment and ethical practices.
  6. Project Management: Use engineering management principles individually, as a team member or a leader to manage projects and effectively communicate about well-dened engineering activities.
  7. Life-long learning: Ability to analyze individual needs and engage in updating in the context of technological changes.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1: The mechanical engineering diploma students acquire technical and managerial skill and software skills that make them and employable technocrat in industries.
  • PSO2: The mechanical engineering diploma students acquire theoretical background of each course that they are capable of applying it for solving real time problems.
