Electrical Engineering

About the Department

The Electrical Engineering Department is dedicated to developing technically strong and industry-ready diploma engineers through a balanced blend of sound theory, practical training, and professional exposure. The department emphasizes outcome-based learning, strong fundamentals in electrical systems, and hands-on skills aligned with modern industrial requirements.

The department is supported by a team of 7 faculty members, including 4 M.Tech. and 3 B.E. qualified teachers. Strengthening the academic and research culture, one faculty member is currently pursuing a Ph.D. from Gujarat Technological University (GTU). This continuous engagement in higher studies and professional development contributes to improved teaching quality and up-to-date technical knowledge.

Beyond classroom and laboratory learning, the department actively promotes knowledge enrichment through expert lectures, technical sessions, and industry interactions. It is also involved in consultancy activities, applying departmental expertise to support technical problem-solving and services for external stakeholders. Through these initiatives, students gain exposure to real-world engineering practices, professional communication, and current industry trends.

The Electrical Engineering Department remains committed to creating a strong academic environment that encourages innovation, discipline, and lifelong learning, preparing students to contribute confidently in industry, higher education, and society.

Department Vision

To transform learners into best technocrat in field of electrical engineering for collective alliance benefit of industry, society and government.

Department Mission

  • M1: Provide quality education by ensuring optimal use of available resources to produce skilled technical personnel in the field of electrical engineering.
  • M2: Implant various technical and soft skills to enrich their problem solving and project management skills
  • M3: Promote organizational and leadership qualities in learners for employability, start ups and Entrepreneurship through various extra-curricular and co-curricular activities.
  • M4: Create an environment where technical personnel will become lifelong learner and ethical servant of Society
  • M5: Develop alliance with industries and other such institute to improve teaching learning, Skill development and placement
