Library

The Institute Library functions as a critical academic resource centre, supporting the dissemination of knowledge through books, journals and digital media.

The library maintains a comprehensive collection of engineering texts relevant to the academic programmes offered by the Institute. Its collection includes academic and non-academic resources, technical and non-technical books, and soft-skill resources, supporting the overall learning needs of students.

The library also provides access to digital learning resources, including e-books, e-journals and NPTEL video lectures. Through integration with the National Digital Library (NDL) and ONOS, students can access an extensive repository of books and research journals.

Library Services

  • Books are issued to students and faculty members as per their requirements, with records maintained for issued books.
  • Newspapers and reference materials are available for in-library use only and are not issued for circulation.

Reading Room

The library has a dedicated reading room with a seating capacity of 60 students, providing a quiet environment for focused reading, study and research.
