    Principal's Message

    It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Government Polytechnic, Chhotaudepur, an institute committed to quality technical education and the development of skilled and responsible technocrats.

    Our vision is to create technically competent professionals who can contribute meaningfully to industry, society and government. We believe that technical education must go beyond classroom learning and provide students with opportunities to develop practical skills, problem-solving abilities, leadership qualities and professional values.
