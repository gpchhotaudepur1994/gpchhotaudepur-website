SSIP – Student Start-up and Innovation Policy

About SSIP

Government Polytechnic, Chhota Udepur encourages students to identify practical problems and develop innovative solutions. The Student Start-up and Innovation Policy (SSIP) of the Government of Gujarat aims to build an innovation and pre-incubation ecosystem that helps students develop and advance their ideas.

Students from all departments are encouraged to share ideas related to engineering, manufacturing, agriculture, energy, water, the environment, assistive technology and community needs. Students may approach the institute’s SSIP coordinator for guidance on developing an idea, preparing a proposal and finding suitable mentors.

Support for Student Ideas

The support described below depends on eligibility, the institute’s participation in SSIP, review of the proposal, available resources and the current policy guidelines.

  • Mentoring and guidance for developing an idea.
  • Help with concept validation and prototype development.
  • Guidance on testing and improving a prototype.
  • Information about intellectual property rights (IPR) support.
  • Guidance on relevant SSIP programmes, events and proposal processes.

Important: Financial assistance or other support is not automatic. Proposals must meet the applicable guidelines and be reviewed through the required process.

How to Submit an Idea

  1. Discuss the idea with a faculty mentor or the SSIP coordinator.
  2. Prepare a brief concept note describing the problem and proposed solution.
  3. Include intended users or beneficiaries, work completed so far, resources required and an estimated budget, if relevant.
  4. Submit the proposal using the current institute or SSIP format.
  5. Present the idea for review if requested, and follow the instructions provided by the institute.

SSIP Activities at the Institute

The institute may publish verified information about its SSIP-related activities on this page. Such information may include:

  • Innovation or entrepreneurship awareness programmes.
  • Idea-generation sessions, workshops and hackathons.
  • Student project presentations and prototype demonstrations.
  • Mentoring sessions and industry interactions.
  • Student proposals, prototypes, IPR support, awards or other outcomes.

For official information about the Student Start-up and Innovation Policy, its programmes and guidelines, visit the SSIP Gujarat portal.
